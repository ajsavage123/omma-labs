## 2. Module 1: The Executive Sales Dashboard

The **Dashboard** (`/crm`) is your morning command center. It gives you a bird’s-eye view of your sales pipeline and immediate priorities.

```
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│  PIPELINE VALUE │ │   CLOSED WON    │ │    DUE TODAY    │ │     OVERDUE     │
│   ₹ 18,50,000   │ │   ₹ 4,20,000    │ │     6 Tasks     │ │     2 Tasks     │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The 4 Stat Cards
* **Pipeline Value (₹)**: The total value of all active potential deals currently in your pipeline (excludes deals marked *Lost* or *Not Interested*).
* **Closed Won (₹)**: Actual revenue closed and won by you/your team, with month-over-month growth trends.
* **Due Today**: Count of calls, demos, or follow-ups scheduled for today (00:00 to 23:59). **Your goal is to get this number to 0 before leaving!**
* **Overdue**: Tasks where the scheduled date and time has passed without being completed. Highlighted in amber/red to prompt immediate action.

### 2.2 Visual Charts
1. **Pipeline Value by Stage (Bar Chart)**: Shows the financial weight in each column of your pipeline (`New Leads`, `Contacted`, `Interested`, `Proposal Sent`, `Negotiation`, `Won`). Look for bottlenecks—if ₹10 Lakhs is stuck in `Proposal Sent`, it’s time to call those prospects!
2. **Lead Distribution (Donut Chart)**: Displays the percentage of your leads across each stage so you know if your pipeline has enough fresh prospects.

### 2.3 Quick-Action Panel
Located at the top right of the dashboard:
* **`+ New Lead`**: Instantly add a new prospect company you discovered.
* **`+ Schedule Task`**: Set a follow-up reminder or client meeting.
* **`📝 Log Note`**: Record an interaction immediately after hanging up a call.

### 4.1 How to Add a New Lead Manually
1. Click the blue **`+ New Lead`** button.
2. Fill out the key fields:
   * **Company Name** *(Required)*: The business name (e.g. "Apex Diagnostics").
   * **Contact Person** *(Required)*: The decision maker (e.g. "Dr. Rajesh Sharma").
   * **Phone Number**: Primary mobile or WhatsApp number.
   * **Email Address**: Official business email.
   * **Stage**: Defaults to `New Leads`.
   * **Service Interest**: What they need (e.g. "Web App", "Mobile App", "AI Automation").
   * **Estimated Deal Value (₹)**: Projected contract value (e.g. `250000`).
   * **Confidence (%)**: Your closing confidence (e.g. `50%`).
   * **Temperature**: Hot 🔥, Warm ☀️, or Cold ❄️.
   * **Website / Location**: Website URL and Google Maps location/address.
3. Click **Save Lead**. It instantly syncs across the whole workspace!

---

### 4.2 Bulk Importing Leads via CSV

You can import hundreds of leads in 10 seconds from Excel or Google Sheets!

1. Click **`📥 Import CSV`**.
2. Select your CSV file (exported from Google Maps scraper, Apollo, Justdial, LinkedIn, or Excel).
3. The built-in **Smart Column Matcher** automatically maps headers even if they are named differently:
   * `"Business Name"`, `"Firm"`, `"Title"`, `"Establishment"` ➔ **Company Name**
   * `"Phone"`, `"Mobile"`, `"Contact No"`, `"Tel"` ➔ **Phone Number**
   * `"Email"`, `"Mail Address"` ➔ **Email**
   * `"Address"`, `"Maps Link"`, `"Location"` ➔ **Address / External Link**
   * `"Website"`, `"URL"`, `"Domain"` ➔ **Website**
4. Any extra columns in your spreadsheet are **never lost**—they are saved into the lead’s `Custom Data` tab for future reference!
5. Click **Confirm Import**.

---

### 4.3 Auto-Repairing "Unknown Company"
If you import a messy CSV where the company name was in an unlabelled column:
* The CRM automatically detects missing names and extracts them from the address or contact name.
* You will never end up with dozens of useless "Unknown" rows.

---

### 4.4 Multi-Select Batch Operations
Need to update 50 leads at once?
1. Check the checkboxes next to the leads (or click the top checkbox to **Select All**).
2. A floating bar appears at the bottom with **Batch Actions**:
   * **Change Stage**: Move all selected leads to `Contacted`, `Interested`, or `Proposal Sent` in 1 click.
   * **Reassign Rep**: Transfer a list of leads to a colleague if someone is on leave.
   * **Batch Delete**: Delete selected leads (Note: Only Admins or the lead creator can delete).

---

### 4.5 1-Click Rollback (Undo an Accidental Import)
Accidentally uploaded the wrong spreadsheet with 500 bad rows? Don’t panic!
1. In the Leads Directory, click **`Import History`**.
2. Locate the file you just imported with its timestamp and row count.
3. Click **`Rollback Batch`**.
4. The CRM safely purges **only** the leads from that specific file without affecting your existing contacts!

---

### 4.6 Finding & Merging Duplicate Leads
Duplicate contacts cause embarrassing double-calls and annoyed prospects.

1. Click **`🔍 Find Duplicates`** in the Leads header.
2. The CRM scans your entire database using 3 rules:
   * **Exact Email Match**
   * **Exact 10-Digit Phone Match** (even if one has `+91` and one has `0`)
   * **Same Company Name + Same Person Name**
3. If duplicates are found, a side-by-side **Merge Modal** opens.
4. Pick which details to keep (the updated phone, correct deal value, etc.).
5. Click **Merge**: Notes and history from both records are combined into one clean profile, and the duplicate is safely removed.

### 5.1 The 7 Pipeline Stages Explained

1. **New Leads**: Uncontacted prospects freshly added or imported.
2. **Contacted**: You have reached out via phone, WhatsApp, or email, but are awaiting a meaningful conversation.
3. **Not Interested**: Prospect explicitly declined at this time (keeps your active pipeline clean).
4. **Interested**: Prospect responded positively, asked questions, or agreed to a meeting.
5. **Proposal Sent**: Formal quotation, scope of work, and commercial pricing have been delivered to the client.
6. **Negotiation**: Final pricing adjustments, contract terms, or milestone schedules are being finalized with decision-makers.
7. **Won (Converted)**: Deal closed! Contract signed, advance payment received.

Every card on the pipeline board gives you instant action capability:

### 5.3 Propensity Lead Score (0 to 100) — What It Means
The CRM runs an automatic mathematical scoring algorithm on every lead. The higher the score, the higher the closing probability!

| Score Range | Badge Color | Meaning & Action |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **80 – 100** | 🟢 Emerald | **Hot Deal**: In late stages with active follow-ups. Focus here to close this week! |
| **50 – 79** | 🟡 Amber | **Warm Prospect**: Meaningful discussions underway. Needs demos & proposals. |
| **20 – 49** | 🔵 Blue | **Early Stage**: In discovery or first outreach. Keep nurturing. |
| **0 – 19** | ⚪ Gray | **Cold / Stalled**: Incomplete details or no response. Needs reactivation. |

#### How You Can Boost a Lead’s Score:
* Advance stage from `New Leads` ➔ `Interested` ➔ `Proposal Sent` (+5 to +35 pts).
* Log calls, WhatsApp touches, and notes (+5 pts per activity, up to +30 pts).
* Complete scheduled tasks on time (+5 pts each).
* Fill in complete data (Phone, Email, Deal Value) (+10 pts).
* Keep recent activity within the last 7 days (+10 pts).
* Moving a deal to **Won (Converted)** instantly awards **100/100**!

### 5.4 Temperature Classification

```
┌──────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────┐
│     HOT 🔥       │  │     WARM ☀️      │  │     COLD ❄️      │
│ Urgent project   │  │ Promising fit    │  │ Low budget /     │
│ Verified budget  │  │ Evaluating       │  │ No current need  │
│ CXO engaged      │  │ Follow-up needed │  │ Long-term nurture│
└──────────────────┘  └──────────────────┘  └──────────────────┘
```

* **Hot 🔥**: Urgent requirement, clear budget > ₹1,50,000, in touch with decision-maker. **Call within 2 hours.**
* **Warm ☀️**: Good fit, interested, but needs approval, scoping demo, or comparison. **Follow up every 2–3 days.**
* **Cold ❄️**: Low budget, non-responsive, or no immediate need. **Place on monthly nurture cadence.**

Take action immediately: call the client or complete the task to clear the alert.

### 6.2 Creating Follow-Ups with Exact Zoho-Style Due Time
Don't just set a date—set an exact hour and minute so you get real-time audio and visual reminders!

1. Click **`+ New Task`**.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

#### Q1: Why is the bell icon oscillating and ringing in the top bar?
> **Answer**: You have a task that is **due right now** or **overdue**. Click the bell icon, view the overdue task, call the client, and click **"Mark Done"** to stop the ringing.

#### Q2: Why can’t I delete a lead?
> **Answer**: To protect the company's valuable sales data, **only the salesperson who originally created the lead** or an **Admin** can delete it. If you believe a lead should be removed, ask your team lead or Admin.

#### Q3: What should I do if a lead says, "I'm busy, call me next Wednesday at 4 PM"?
> **Answer**: 
> 1. Open the lead card.
> 2. Click the **`+ Schedule Task`** button.
> 3. Select next Wednesday's date and enter `16:00` (4:00 PM).
> 4. Title it: *"Follow-up call with [Name] as requested"*.
> 5. Log a quick note: *"Client was busy in a meeting; asked to call Wednesday 4pm."*
> 6. The CRM will automatically alarm you next Wednesday at 3:59 PM!

#### Q4: How does the 1-click WhatsApp button work?
> **Answer**: Make sure the phone number in the lead has a valid mobile number (10 digits). Clicking the green WhatsApp icon opens WhatsApp Web on your PC (or your WhatsApp app on mobile) with a conversation opened directly to that client!

#### Q5: What happens when I mark a lead as "Won (Converted)"?
> **Answer**: Congratulations on closing the deal! Three things happen immediately:
> 1. The lead’s Propensity Score jumps to **100/100**.
> 2. The deal value is credited to your Closed Won revenue on the Dashboard.
> 3. An onboarding project card is automatically created in the **Projects** workspace so the engineering team can begin delivery!

#### Q6: How is my performance evaluated on Day 7?
> **Answer**: Your manager reviews the **Live CRM Audit**, which verifies how many actual leads you added, calls/messages you logged, meetings you conducted, and deals you progressed. Keeping your CRM updated daily guarantees an **Approved** rating!
